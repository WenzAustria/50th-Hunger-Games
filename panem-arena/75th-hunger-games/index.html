75th Hunger Games

No victor

The Games ended without a victor

Description

The Third Quarter Quell reaped the tributes from the existing victors of each district. The arena was a giant clock with a different danger in each of its twelve sectors. A rebel plan broke the arena apart, so no victor was ever crowned. Six tributes survived, and these were the last Hunger Games ever held.
