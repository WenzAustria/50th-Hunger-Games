49th Hunger Games

Wiress

District 3 — Technology

Description

Wiress of District 3 was famous for her technical brilliance and her broken, quiet way of speaking. The stories do not describe how she won. In the 75th Games she realised the arena was a clock, an insight that helped her allies before she was killed.
