34th Hunger Games

Beetee Latier

District 3 — Technology

Description

Beetee Latier of District 3 won by intellect rather than strength. Using parts scavenged from his arena, he built an electrical trap; according to Haymitch he electrocuted six tributes at once. Years later his technical skill helped the rebels in the 75th Games.
