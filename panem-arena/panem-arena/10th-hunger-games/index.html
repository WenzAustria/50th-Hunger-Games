10th Hunger Games

Lucy Gray Baird

District 12 — Coal Mining

Description

The 10th Hunger Games took place in a crumbling, bomb-damaged Capitol amphitheater. District 12 tribute Lucy Gray Baird was mentored by an 18-year-old Coriolanus Snow. She survived five days by hiding, outlasting rivals, winning sponsor gifts and using the snakes and a hidden vial of poison, and became the first victor from District 12.
