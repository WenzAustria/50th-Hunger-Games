About the 50th Hunger Games

The Second Quarter Quell doubled the tributes: 48 children, two boys and two girls from each of the 12 districts, were sent into a beautiful but deadly arena of poisonous plants, mutts and an active volcano.

Victor: Haymitch Abernathy, District 12. He turned the arena itself against the Capitol and became the only living victor from his district until the 74th Games.

How to play
Keyboard + mouse
WASD move • Mouse look (click to capture) • Left click attack • Shift sprint • Space dash • E pick up / drink / eat • 1-2-3 weapon slots • Q cycle • H medkit • T autopilot • P pause • M mute
Phone / tablet
Left stick moves • Drag the right side to look • ATK attacks • USE picks up, eats, drinks • DASH, MED and RUN buttons • Tap slots to switch weapons. Landscape works best.
Weapons
🔪 Melee: knife, spear, mace
🏹 Ranged: bow, throwing knives, slingshot
🪓 Survival: axe, sword, trident, blowgun & darts, sickle
Survive
Rush the Cornucopia for weapons. Drink at ponds, eat orange fruit (never purple berries). Stay inside the safe zone and dodge volcano fireballs. Last tribute standing wins.
