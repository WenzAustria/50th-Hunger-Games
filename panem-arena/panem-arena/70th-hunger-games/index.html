70th Hunger Games

Annie Cresta

District 4 — Fishing

Description

Annie Cresta of District 4 won the 70th Games after the arena flooded; her swimming skill kept her alive. She was deeply traumatised after seeing her district partner beheaded, and the experience affected her mind for years.
