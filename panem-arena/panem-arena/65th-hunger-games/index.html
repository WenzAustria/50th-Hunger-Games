Finnick Odair

District 4 — Fishing

Description

Finnick Odair of District 4 won the 65th Games while still a young teenager, using a trident and net. The Capitol then exploited his fame and looks. He became a loyal ally of Katniss and a rebel, and was Annie Cresta's true love.
