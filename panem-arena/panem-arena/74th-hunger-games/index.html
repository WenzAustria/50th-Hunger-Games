74th Hunger Games

Katniss Everdeen & Peeta Mellark

District 12 — Coal Mining

Description

Katniss Everdeen volunteered to take her sister Prim's place. When the rule allowing two victors was revoked, she and Peeta Mellark threatened to eat poisonous berries together. The Gamemakers stepped in and declared both of them victors, the first pair in the history of the Games.
